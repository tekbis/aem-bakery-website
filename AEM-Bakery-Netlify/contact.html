Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Email AEMBakery@gmail.com.

How far ahead should I order?

Everything is baked fresh to order, so please give us at least 6 hours. For a big order or a specific day, a day or two ahead is best.

How do I pay?

Add bakes to your cart and check out on this site. You pay securely online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay when you place your order.

What about allergies and ingredients?

Please get in touch before ordering so we can talk through your needs. Our bakes are made in a home kitchen, so don’t assume a product is suitable for a specific allergy or diet.
