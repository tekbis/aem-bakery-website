How holiday baskets work

Each basket starts at $5.00 for the basket itself. Fill it with our holiday basket items, like homemade vanilla bean paste, caramel and infused honey. Each item is charged at its listed price.

Build as many baskets as you like, each with its own contents. Choose pickup in Ramsey or delivery. Orders can be scheduled from November 1 to December 12, 2026, and baskets are ready December 20–23.
